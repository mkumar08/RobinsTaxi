WHAT CUSTOMERS SAY

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What areas does Robin's Taxi serve?

Robin's Taxi serves Middletown, NY and all of Orange County, including Goshen, Monroe, Newburgh, Wallkill, Chester, Warwick, and Port Jervis. We also provide long-distance service to New York City and surrounding areas, including all major airports.

Is Robin's Taxi available 24 hours a day?

Yes. We operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays. Call 845-343-5555 at any hour to book a ride.

Do you provide airport transfers from Middletown?

Yes. We provide direct airport transfers from Middletown and the surrounding Orange County area to JFK, LaGuardia, Newark Liberty, and Stewart International. Reservations are recommended for early-morning flights.

How far in advance should I book an airport ride?

We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance to guarantee a vehicle, especially for early-morning or holiday travel. Last-minute bookings are accepted based on availability.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, credit cards, and debit cards. Payment is collected at the end of your ride.

Do you have larger vehicles for groups?

Yes. Our fleet includes sedans, SUVs, and passenger vans for groups and families. Let us know the number of passengers when you book and we will send the right vehicle.

How do I get a fare quote?

Call 845-343-5555 with your pickup and drop-off locations. We provide upfront flat-rate quotes for airport trips and long-distance rides so there are no surprises.

Can I book a ride to a NYC sports event or concert?

Yes. We regularly drive customers to Yankee Stadium, Citi Field, Madison Square Garden, MetLife Stadium, and major concert venues throughout the NYC metro area.

Do you provide non-emergency medical transportation?

Yes. We provide non-emergency medical transportation (NEMT) to and from medical appointments, dialysis centers, and hospitals across Orange County. Use the Arrange Medical Transportation link in our footer to start a reservation.

Are your drivers licensed and insured?

Yes. All Robin's Taxi vehicles are commercially licensed and insured, and our drivers are trained, vetted, and experienced with local roads and routes to NYC.

Do you offer wedding or special event transportation?

Yes. We offer hourly and event-based car service for weddings, proms, anniversaries, and corporate events.

Can I schedule a return pickup from the airport?

Yes. Schedule both your departure and return airport trips at the same time. We track your flight and adjust pickup time if your flight is delayed.
